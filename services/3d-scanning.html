No drawing? No problem.

When the spare part has no CAD, no drawing and no supplier left, we scan the physical object, rebuild it as a clean model and print a replacement. Ideal for obsolete components, legacy tooling and one-off fixtures.

Send a part enquiry WhatsApp us

What reverse engineering actually involves

A scan is a dense point cloud — accurate, but not a usable mechanical model. The real work is rebuilding it: establishing datums, straightening features that were never meant to be straight, adding wall thickness where the scan can’t see, and recreating threads, bores and fits as clean geometry.

Where this helps most

  • Obsolete spares with no drawings and no supplier
  • Legacy tooling, jigs and fixtures that need a duplicate
  • Hand-made parts that need to become repeatable
  • Existing products being redesigned or repackaged
  • Quality inspection against a reference part

Scan, rebuild, print — or just scan

Some clients only need the CAD file. Others need the physical replacement. We quote both separately so you can stop at whichever stage you need.

What we need from you

Ship or drop off the part. If it is still in service, send photos first — we will tell you whether scanning is the right route or whether a drawing would be faster and cheaper.

Have a part with no drawing?

Send photos and dimensions. We will confirm feasibility and quote the scan, the CAD rebuild or both.
