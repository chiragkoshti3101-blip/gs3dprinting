Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GS3D Printing (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit gs3dprinting.com or use our services.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number and company name submitted through our enquiry forms.
  • Project information: details, drawings and files you share about your 3D printing or manufacturing requirements.
  • Technical data: browser type, device information and pages visited, where analytics is enabled.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotations.
  • To deliver the services you have requested.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We share data only with service providers who help us deliver our services (under confidentiality agreements) or with legal authorities where required by law.

5. Confidentiality of project files

Design files, drawings and models you send are used only to quote and produce your project. We do not reuse, resell or publicly display them without your written permission. We will sign a non-disclosure agreement on request, before files are shared.

6. Data security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

8. Your rights

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

9. Cookies and analytics

This website does not load advertising trackers or third-party pixels by default. If analytics is enabled by the site operator, it is used solely to understand page traffic, and you can block cookies through your browser settings.

10. Third-party services

Enquiry forms hand your details to WhatsApp when you choose to send them, so that conversation is also governed by WhatsApp’s privacy policy. Links to Google Maps are provided for location reference.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.

12. Contact us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, email gs3dprinting@gmail.com or call +91 90997 19967.
