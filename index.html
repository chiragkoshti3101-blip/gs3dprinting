    Three processes, chosen for the job

    We don’t push one machine for everything. Detail work goes to resin, functional parts to filament or nylon — and we tell you which and why before you approve the quote.

    SLA

    Stereolithography

    Smooth, high-detail resin parts. The default choice for presentation models and fine features.

    Typical layer
    0.025–0.1 mm
    Typical tolerance
    ±0.1 mm
    Materials
    Standard, tough, clear resin
    Best for
    Detail, finish, models
    FDM

    Fused Deposition Modeling

    Tough, cost-effective parts built layer by layer. Good for jigs, fixtures and early functional tests.

    Typical layer
    0.1–0.3 mm
    Typical tolerance
    ±0.3 mm
    Materials
    PLA, ABS, PETG, TPU
    Best for
    Function, size, value
    SLS

    Selective Laser Sintering

    Strong nylon parts with no visible layer lines and no support marks. Suits complex, end-use geometry.

    Typical layer
    0.08–0.12 mm
    Typical tolerance
    ±0.3%
    Materials
    PA12 nylon
    Best for
    Complex, durable parts

    Values are typical process characteristics, not a guarantee for every geometry. Thin walls, long parts and tight fits behave differently — send the file and we will confirm achievable tolerance in the quote.

    What actually drives the price

    Custom manufacturing can’t have a fixed price list — but it shouldn’t be a mystery either. These are the six variables in every GS3D quote.

    Part volume

    Material and machine time scale with size. A 200 mm part is not double a 100 mm part — it is often triple.

    Process & resolution

    Resin at 0.025 mm costs more per part than filament at 0.2 mm. We quote the cheapest process that meets the requirement.

    Material

    Standard resin and PLA sit at one end; engineering nylon, TPU and clear resin at the other.

    Finish

    Raw print, sanded, primed, painted, clear-coated or assembled. Finishing is usually the largest labour line on a display model.

    Quantity

    One-offs carry setup cost. Repeat batches share it — the tenth part is much cheaper than the first.

    Deadline

    Standard scheduling is planned around print queue. Exhibition dates can be prioritised — tell us the date up front.

    What clients say after delivery

    Questions we get before every order

    How quickly will I get a quote?
    Send your file or drawing and we reply within 24 working hours. Complex assemblies may take one extra day while we check print orientation, split lines and finish.
    What files should I send?
    STL or 3MF for printing; STEP or IGES if you have solid models; PDF or DWG drawings when tolerances matter. If you only have a sketch, photos or a sample part, we can model it for you.
    How large a model can you make?
    Large models are built in sections and assembled — our exhibition models include multi-level plant layouts well over a metre wide. Send the footprint and we will confirm the split and the crate size.
    Which material should I choose?
    Resin (SLA) for fine detail and smooth surfaces; filament (FDM) for tough, economical or large parts; nylon (SLS) for complex functional parts without visible layer lines. We recommend one in the quote rather than leaving it to you.
    Do you deliver outside Ahmedabad?
    Yes. We ship across India and have served clients in 15 cities. Models travel in custom foam crates with tracking, and we can coordinate installation for large exhibition pieces.
    Can you sign an NDA?
    Yes — tell us at enquiry stage and we will sign before files are shared. Design files remain yours and are not reused for other clients.
    Who owns the design files?
    You do. We keep working files for the duration of the project and for reprints you request; we do not resell or display your design without permission.
    Can you match a colour or finish?
    Yes. We sand, prime, paint to RAL or Pantone references and clear-coat. Send a physical sample or a reference image and we will match as closely as the substrate allows.
    Do you handle assembly and installation?
    For exhibition and architectural models, yes — removable parts, alignment pins, lighting and on-site assembly guidance are part of the scope we quote.
    Is there a minimum order?
    No. Single prototype parts are welcome, and repeat batches are priced to share setup cost.
